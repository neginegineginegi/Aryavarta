Handoff 20 — the eight new sections

Static reference build. Every section below is the approved design, rendered from globals-additions.css — no app code, no scroll reveal, no animation. Each label states where the section goes in page.tsx.

Sections that already exist on the page (hero, features, stats, network, timeline, on this day, about, ledger, FAQ, open data, CTA, footer) are not shown here and must not be changed.

01 WHO IT’S FOR — after archive features, before stats
WHO IT’S FOR

One record,
read differently by everyone.

◈
Researchers
Cross-reference officeholders, elections and events without re-deriving them from primary sources each time.
✎
Journalists
Verify who held office when, on deadline, with a citation ready to publish.
▤
Students
See a period of Indian political history laid out year by year, with sources attached.
◎
Genealogists & local historians
Trace who governed a state or district across the years a family or place was there.

ASK THE ARCHIVE

plain language.

Type a question the way you’d ask a person. The answer comes back with the office, the dates, and the source.

Punjab was under President’s Rule for all of 1987.
