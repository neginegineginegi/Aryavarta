CONTRIBUTE

How a correction
lands.

No account, no edit wars. One path from claim to record.

01

Submit with a source

Propose the change and paste the published source it comes from. A name is all that’s asked.

02

A named moderator reviews

The claim is checked against the source. What can’t be verified is returned, not published.

03

It joins the record

The entry updates with your name on it, and the previous version stays in the public history.

Submit a correction
